# Adopters

Who uses originshift, as far as I know.

The package reports nothing. There is no telemetry, no phone-home, and no
analytics, and there never will be — a tool that reads regulations for a
compliance decision has no business also reporting what you looked up. That
design means the list below is the only way I learn anything, and it is
maintained by hand.

## Why it is worth telling me

Knowing who relies on this changes what I maintain. A rule table nobody queries
and one carried in a filing workflow deserve different care, and I cannot tell
them apart from download counts. If a corpus you depend on is about to change
under an eCFR amendment, I would rather warn you than let you find out.

## How to be listed

Open an issue with the **Adopter** template, or send a pull request adding a
row below. Either is fine.

**If you would rather not be public, email instead.** A trade-compliance team's
tooling can be commercially sensitive, and a private note is as useful to me as
a public row. Say so and it stays private — I will not list you.
